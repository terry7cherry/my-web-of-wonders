Base Game

  • Played on a 5×5 grid with Pays Anywhere mechanics.
  • The Spider occupies one corner of the grid and defines the active 3x3 collection area.
  • Only Coins landing within this area pay out.
  • Coins outside the area do not pay.
  • The Spider might relocate to a new corner at the start of each spin.
  • WILD symbols substitute for all regular symbols to complete winning combinations.
  • Bonus (Egg) symbols:
    • 3 symbols anywhere on the grid trigger Eggwakening.
    • If fewer than 3 land, Bonus symbols within the active Spider area are collected into the Bonus pot.
  • Filling the Bonus pot triggers Super Eggwakening.
  • Cascades occur after wins and Coin collections, allowing new symbols to fall into place.

Eggwakening

  • Triggered by landing 3 Bonus (Egg) symbols anywhere on the grid.
  • The Spider becomes static for the duration of the feature.
  • The Spider's collection area starts with a x2 multiplier applied to Coin payouts.

Eggs & Areas

  • 3 Eggs are placed in fixed positions on the grid.
  • Each Egg defines its own collection area.
  • Areas are not uniform and may partially overlap visually.
  • If multiple areas affect the same position, only the highest multiplier applies.

Coins

  • Coins in active Spider/Baby Spider areas:
    • Pay out and are multiplied.
  • Coins in Egg areas:
    • Do not pay.
    • Instead are collected and contribute to the Egg's growth meter.

Hatching

  • When an Egg is filled, it hatches into a Baby Spider.
  • Each hatch:
    • Activates that area for Coin payouts.
    • Increases the main Spider multiplier by +1.
    • Increases all existing Baby Spider multipliers by +1.
    • Retriggers additional feature spins.

Super Eggwakening

  • Triggered by overflowing the Bonus pot in the base game.
  • All Eggwakening rules apply, with the following enhancements:

Starting Advantage

  • 1–3 Eggs hatch immediately at the start of the feature.
  • This creates instant active payout areas and higher starting multipliers.

Full Growth State

  • If all 3 Eggs hatch during the feature:
    • All grid positions become x5.
